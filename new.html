Featured Research Summary

Ecofeminist Approaches in Dystopian Narratives: The Domination of Nature and Women

Critical Monograph • Aldous Huxley's Brave New World • Theoretical Framework: Karen J. Warren[cite: 1]

Abstract: This study explores the structural intersection between patriarchal control and environmental exploitation in dystopian literature[cite: 1]. Utilizing Karen J. Warren's concepts of the "logic of domination" and oppositional value dualisms, the paper examines how the World State establishes totalizing stability by replacing natural reproduction with mechanized technology and suppressing organic ecological ties[cite: 1].
  • Technological Subjugation: Eradication of maternal biological autonomy via the Bokanovsky Process to secure the artificial hegemony of culture over nature[cite: 1].
  • Bodily Reduction: Social conditioning of female subjectivity into commodified, utilitarian "pneumatic" objects[cite: 1].
  • Emotional Containment: The mandatory reliance on soma as chemical pacification that prevents critical consciousness and authentic feeling[cite: 1].

* Monograph prepared for academic exchange. Full text, complete theoretical references, and citation data are open upon scholarly inquiry[cite: 1].
